W1 Business Intelligence in the AI Age ▾

Learning outcomes

  • Build an automated ETL pipeline with dbt and BigQuery
  • Set up real-time data quality monitoring
W4 Advanced BI Dashboards & Self-Service ▾

Topics covered

  • Power BI Copilot and Tableau Pulse features

Topics covered
