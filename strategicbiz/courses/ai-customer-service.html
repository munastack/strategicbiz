Topics covered
W1 AI in the Contact Centre ▾

Topics covered

  • Evolution of customer service: IVR to GPT agents
  • Omnichannel AI: chat, voice, email, and social
  • Key platforms: Zendesk AI, Intercom Fin, Salesforce Einstein
  • Metrics: CSAT, NPS, AHT, FCR, and containment rate

Learning outcomes

  • Audit a customer service operation for AI opportunities
  • Benchmark three AI customer service platforms
W2 Intelligent Ticketing & Triage ▾

Topics covered

  • Automatic ticket classification and routing
  • Priority scoring with sentiment and urgency detection
  • AI-suggested responses and agent assist tools
  • Integration with Zendesk, Freshdesk, and Jira Service

Learning outcomes

  • Configure AI ticket routing for a sample help desk
  • Train a sentiment-based priority model
W3 Conversational AI for Customer Service ▾

Topics covered

  • Building service chatbots with Dialogflow and RASA
  • Escalation logic: bot to human handoff
  • Multilingual support with AI translation
  • Voice bots and IVR replacement with conversational AI

Learning outcomes

  • Build a customer service bot handling 20+ intents
  • Implement seamless bot-to-agent escalation
W4 Agent Assist & Knowledge Management ▾

Topics covered

  • Real-time agent suggestions and next-best-action
  • AI knowledge base creation and auto-updating
  • After-call work automation: summaries and CRM updates
  • Quality assurance with AI call and chat analysis

Learning outcomes

  • Deploy an agent assist system with knowledge retrieval
  • Build an AI-powered QA scoring system
W5 Personalisation & Proactive Service ▾

Topics covered

  • Customer 360 profiles and AI-driven personalisation
  • Predictive churn detection and retention outreach
  • Proactive notifications and outbound AI campaigns
  • Personalised self-service portals

Learning outcomes

  • Build a churn prediction model with 80%+ accuracy
  • Design a proactive outreach campaign using AI triggers
W6 Voice AI & Conversational Analytics ▾

Topics covered

  • Speech-to-text and voice sentiment analysis
  • Emotion detection in customer calls
  • Topic modelling across support interactions
  • Compliance monitoring and PII redaction

Learning outcomes

  • Analyse 100 call transcripts for emerging issues
  • Implement PII redaction on customer data
W7 CX Data Analytics & Reporting ▾

Topics covered

  • Building customer service dashboards with AI
  • Root cause analysis of recurring complaints
  • Cohort analysis of customer satisfaction trends
  • Presenting AI insights to business stakeholders

Learning outcomes

  • Build a real-time CX analytics dashboard
  • Deliver a data-backed CX improvement proposal
W8 Capstone: AI CX Transformation ▾

Topics covered

  • Designing an AI-first customer service strategy
  • Change management and agent re-skilling plans
  • ROI calculation: cost-per-contact and deflection rates
  • Final project: full AI CX transformation blueprint

Learning outcomes

  • Present a complete AI CX strategy to a panel
  • Demonstrate measurable improvement in simulation KPIs
