About SBT

Fifteen years of building Nigeria's AI talent pipeline.

Strategic Business Techspace (SBT) was founded in 2011 with a focus on AI solutions and research certification. SBT was the first indigenous company to offer a certification program in AI chatbot engineering, known as CAiBotD, and is an industry member of the Canadian AI Association.

Beyond training, SBT has built AI chatbot solutions for organisations across sectors, and facilitated STEM robotics training for the south-western states under the Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs.

  • Accredited courses — all 11 programs in this series are fully accredited.
  • Paid internship — successful graduates move into a paid internship before job placement.
  • Job placement support — guaranteed placement support and career advisory after internship.
  • AI-powered learning — every course is taught using the same AI tools learners will use on the job.
